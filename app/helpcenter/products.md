# Standing desk: pairing the control panel
<!-- id: desk-reset -->
If the Rise standing desk shows E01 or will not move, reset it: unplug the desk for 30 seconds, plug it back in, then hold the down button until the desk reaches its lowest point and beeps. The desk is ready to use after the beep.

# Standing desk weight limit and height range
<!-- id: desk-specs -->
The Rise standing desk supports up to 120 kg (265 lb) and adjusts from 72 cm to 120 cm in height. The desktop measures 140 cm by 70 cm.

# Standing desk assembly
<!-- id: desk-assembly -->
Assembling the Rise standing desk takes about 40 minutes with two people. All tools are included except a Phillips screwdriver.

# Chair: adjusting lumbar support
<!-- id: chair-lumbar -->
On the Tern task chair, turn the dial on the right side of the backrest to increase or decrease lumbar support, and slide the lumbar pad up or down to set its height.

# Chair sinking
<!-- id: chair-sinking -->
If your Tern task chair slowly sinks, the gas lift may be faulty. Check that the height lever returns fully to its resting position. If the chair still sinks, make a warranty claim and we will send a replacement gas lift.

# Chair care
<!-- id: chair-care -->
Vacuum the Tern chair's mesh weekly and wipe plastic parts with a damp cloth. Do not use bleach or alcohol cleaners on the mesh.

# Monitor arm compatibility
<!-- id: monitor-arm -->
The Perch monitor arm fits screens up to 34 inches and 9 kg (19.8 lb) with a VESA 75 x 75 mm or 100 x 100 mm mount. It clamps to desktops between 1 cm and 6 cm thick.

# Desk lamp controls
<!-- id: lamp-controls -->
The Glim desk lamp has 5 brightness levels and 3 colour temperatures. Hold the power button for 2 seconds to switch the night light on.

# Wireless keyboard pairing
<!-- id: keyboard-pairing -->
To pair the Quill wireless keyboard, switch it on, then hold Fn and 1, 2 or 3 for 3 seconds until the light blinks, and select Quill Keyboard in your device's Bluetooth settings. It remembers up to 3 devices.

# Keyboard battery life
<!-- id: keyboard-battery -->
The Quill keyboard battery lasts up to 3 months with the backlight off and about 2 weeks with it on. It charges over USB-C in 2 hours.

# Headset microphone not working
<!-- id: headset-mic -->
If the Murmur headset microphone is silent, check that the mute switch on the left ear cup is off, select the headset as the input device in your computer's sound settings, and update the firmware with the Murmur app.

# Headset firmware update
<!-- id: headset-firmware -->
Update Murmur headset firmware with the Murmur app on Windows or macOS. Connect the headset with its USB cable, keep it plugged in, and expect the update to take about 5 minutes.

# Webcam setup
<!-- id: webcam-setup -->
The Peek webcam records 1080p video at 30 frames per second. Plug it into a USB port and it works without drivers on Windows, macOS and ChromeOS.

# Cleaning desktops
<!-- id: desktop-care -->
Wipe Fernwick desktops with a soft damp cloth and dry them straight away. Use coasters for hot mugs, and avoid abrasive cleaners.
