# Support hours and contact
<!-- id: support-hours -->
Our support team replies Monday to Friday, 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM Eastern Time, and Saturday 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM. We are closed on Sundays. Tickets are usually answered within 1 business day.

# Business and bulk orders
<!-- id: bulk-orders -->
Orders of 10 or more units of the same item qualify for business pricing. Send the product names, quantities and delivery location to support, and a quote is sent within 2 business days.

# Recycling old equipment
<!-- id: recycling -->
When you buy a Fernwick chair or desk, we can collect and recycle your old one for $19. Add the Recycle old furniture option at checkout.

# Showroom
<!-- id: showroom -->
Fernwick Goods sells online only. We do not have a showroom or physical store.

# Accessibility
<!-- id: accessibility -->
Our website supports keyboard navigation and screen readers. If something is hard to use, tell support and we can place the order with you by phone.
