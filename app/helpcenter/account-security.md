# Resetting your password
<!-- id: reset-password -->
Select Forgot password on the sign-in page and enter your email address. The reset link is valid for 30 minutes. If no email arrives, check your spam folder and make sure you used the address on the account.

# Changing your email address
<!-- id: change-email -->
Go to Account, then Profile, and edit your email address. We send a confirmation link to the new address, and the change takes effect only after you select it.

# Two-step verification
<!-- id: two-factor -->
Turn on two-step verification under Account, then Security. You can use an authenticator app or text message codes. Save the 10 recovery codes shown during setup, because each code works once if you lose your phone.

# Locked accounts
<!-- id: locked-account -->
An account is locked for 15 minutes after 5 failed sign-in attempts. Wait 15 minutes or reset your password to unlock it right away.

# Guest checkout
<!-- id: guest-checkout -->
You can order without an account. Guest orders can be tracked with the link in the order email. Creating an account later with the same email address adds earlier guest orders to your history.

# Closing your account
<!-- id: close-account -->
You can close your account under Account, then Privacy, then Close account. Closing is permanent after 30 days, during which signing in cancels the request. Unused store credit is lost when the account is permanently closed.

# Email preferences
<!-- id: email-preferences -->
Unsubscribe from marketing using the link at the bottom of any marketing email, or under Account, then Notifications. You will still receive order and security emails.

# Spotting phishing messages
<!-- id: phishing -->
Fernwick Goods never asks for your password or full card number by email, text or phone. Official emails come from addresses ending in @fernwick.example. If you receive a suspicious message, do not click its links; forward it to security@fernwick.example.

# Downloading your data
<!-- id: data-download -->
Request a copy of your account data under Account, then Privacy, then Download my data. The file is emailed within 7 days.

# Saved addresses
<!-- id: saved-addresses -->
You can store up to 10 delivery addresses under Account, then Addresses, and mark one as the default.
