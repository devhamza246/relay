# Return window
<!-- id: return-window -->
Most items can be returned within 30 days of delivery. Fernwick Plus members have 60 days. Items must be unused or lightly used, with all parts and accessories.

# How to start a return
<!-- id: start-return -->
Open Account, then Orders, select the item and choose Return. Print the prepaid label, pack the item, attach the label and drop the parcel at any carrier location. You do not need to contact support to start a normal return.

# Return shipping fees
<!-- id: return-fees -->
Returns are free for faulty, damaged or incorrect items. For other returns, a $6.95 label fee is deducted from your refund. The fee is waived for Fernwick Plus members and when you choose store credit.

# Refund timing
<!-- id: refund-timing -->
We inspect returns within 2 business days of arrival and then issue the refund to your original payment method. Banks usually take 5 to 10 business days to show it. Store credit is available immediately after inspection.

# Items that cannot be returned
<!-- id: non-returnable -->
Gift cards, downloadable software, personalised or engraved items, and opened hygiene items such as ear tips cannot be returned. Clearance items marked Final Sale cannot be returned unless they arrive faulty.

# Damaged on arrival
<!-- id: damaged-item -->
If an item arrives damaged, report it within 7 days of delivery with a photo of the item and the packaging. We will send a free replacement or give a full refund, your choice. Keep the packaging until the claim is closed.

# Wrong item received
<!-- id: wrong-item -->
If you received the wrong item, report it within 14 days of delivery. We ship the correct item right away and send a free prepaid label for the wrong one. You have 14 days to send the wrong item back.

# Exchanges
<!-- id: exchanges -->
To exchange an item for a different size or colour, start a return and place a new order for the item you want. This is the fastest way, because it does not wait for the return to arrive.

# Returning a gift
<!-- id: gift-returns -->
Gift recipients can return an item with the order number from the packing slip. The refund is issued as store credit to the recipient, and the buyer is not notified.

# Warranty coverage
<!-- id: warranty -->
Fernwick-branded furniture has a 5 year warranty and Fernwick-branded electronics have a 2 year warranty against manufacturing defects. Warranties do not cover accidental damage, normal wear or unauthorised repairs. Other brands are covered by their maker's warranty.

# Making a warranty claim
<!-- id: warranty-claim -->
To claim under warranty, send support your order number, a short description of the fault and a photo or video. We reply within 3 business days with a repair, replacement part, or replacement item.
