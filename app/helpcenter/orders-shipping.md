# Shipping options and costs
<!-- id: shipping-options -->
Fernwick Goods offers three delivery options in the continental US. Standard shipping takes 3 to 5 business days and costs $5.95. Express shipping takes 2 business days and costs $14.95. Overnight shipping arrives the next business day and costs $29.95.

Standard shipping is free on orders of $50 or more, after discounts.

# Order cut-off times
<!-- id: order-cutoff -->
Orders placed before 2:00 PM Eastern Time on a business day are packed and shipped the same day. Orders placed after that time, or on a weekend or public holiday, ship the next business day.

# Tracking your package
<!-- id: track-package -->
We email a tracking link as soon as your parcel leaves our warehouse. You can also open Account, then Orders, and select Track next to the order. Tracking information can take up to 24 hours to appear after the shipping email.

# Changing or cancelling an order
<!-- id: change-cancel-order -->
You can change or cancel an order yourself within 60 minutes of placing it. Open Account, then Orders, and select Edit or Cancel. After 60 minutes the order has usually gone to the warehouse and can no longer be changed; you can return the items once they arrive.

# Changing the delivery address
<!-- id: change-address -->
The delivery address can be edited within 60 minutes of ordering from Account, then Orders. After the parcel has shipped, we cannot redirect it, but you may be able to redirect it through the carrier's tracking page.

# International shipping
<!-- id: international-shipping -->
We ship to Canada, the United Kingdom, Ireland and Australia. International delivery takes 7 to 14 business days and costs $24.95 per order. Import duties and taxes are not included in the price and are paid by the recipient on delivery. We do not ship to other countries.

# PO boxes and military addresses
<!-- id: po-box -->
Standard shipping can be sent to PO boxes and to APO, FPO and DPO military addresses. Express and overnight shipping are not available for these addresses.

# Late or missing deliveries
<!-- id: late-delivery -->
If tracking shows no movement for 3 business days, or your parcel is more than 3 business days past the estimated date, contact support and we will open a carrier trace. A trace takes up to 5 business days. If the carrier confirms the parcel is lost, we send a replacement or refund the order.

# Marked delivered but not received
<!-- id: delivered-not-received -->
If tracking says delivered but you cannot find the parcel, check around the property, with neighbours and with your building's mailroom, then wait 1 business day, because carriers sometimes mark parcels early. If it still has not appeared, report it within 14 days of the delivery date so we can file a claim.

# Split shipments
<!-- id: split-shipments -->
Large orders may ship from two warehouses and arrive in separate parcels on different days. You receive one tracking link per parcel and you are charged the shipping fee only once.

# Gift orders
<!-- id: gift-orders -->
You can add gift wrapping for $4.95 per item at checkout and include a free gift message of up to 200 characters. Packing slips for gift orders do not show prices.

# Pre-orders and backorders
<!-- id: backorders -->
Backordered and pre-order items show an estimated ship date on the product page. Your card is charged when the item ships, not when you order. You can cancel a backordered item at any time before it ships.
