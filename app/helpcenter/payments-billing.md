# Accepted payment methods
<!-- id: payment-methods -->
We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover, Apple Pay, Google Pay, PayPal and Fernwick gift cards. We do not accept personal checks, cash on delivery or cryptocurrency.

# When your card is charged
<!-- id: when-charged -->
Your card is authorised when you place the order and charged when the order ships. A pending authorisation is not a charge and drops off your statement within 3 to 7 business days if the order is cancelled.

# Updating your saved card
<!-- id: update-card -->
Go to Account, then Payment methods, and select Add card or Remove. To change the card used for your Fernwick Plus renewal, set the new card as Default.

# Why a payment was declined
<!-- id: payment-declined -->
Payments are usually declined because the billing address does not match the one held by the bank, the card has expired, or the bank has blocked the purchase. Check the details, try another card or payment method, or ask your bank to approve the transaction.

# Using gift cards
<!-- id: gift-cards -->
Enter the 16 digit gift card code at checkout. Gift cards never expire and have no fees. Any remaining balance stays on the card, and you can pay the rest of an order with another method. You can use up to 3 gift cards on one order.

# Promo codes
<!-- id: promo-codes -->
Only one promo code can be used per order, and codes cannot be added after the order has been placed. Codes do not apply to gift cards or to Final Sale items.

# Price adjustments
<!-- id: price-adjustment -->
If the price of an item drops within 14 days of your purchase, contact support and we refund the difference as store credit. Price adjustments do not apply to clearance, lightning deals or other retailers' prices.

# Sales tax
<!-- id: sales-tax -->
Sales tax is calculated from the delivery address and shown at checkout before you pay. Tax-exempt organisations can apply by uploading an exemption certificate under Account, then Tax settings; review takes 2 business days.

# Invoices and receipts
<!-- id: invoices -->
Download a PDF invoice for any order from Account, then Orders, then Invoice. You can add a company name and tax ID to future invoices under Account, then Billing details.

# Buy now, pay later
<!-- id: installments -->
Orders between $75 and $1,500 can be split into 4 interest-free payments taken every 2 weeks. The first payment is due at checkout. Approval depends on the payment partner's checks. Gift cards cannot be bought this way.

# Store credit
<!-- id: store-credit -->
Store credit is applied automatically at checkout. It does not expire, cannot be exchanged for cash and cannot be transferred to another account.
