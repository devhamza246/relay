# What Fernwick Plus includes
<!-- id: plus-benefits -->
Fernwick Plus includes free express shipping on all orders, 60 day returns with no label fee, 5% back in store credit on Fernwick-branded items, and early access to sales.

# Fernwick Plus price
<!-- id: plus-price -->
Fernwick Plus costs $49 per year or $5.99 per month.

# Free trial
<!-- id: plus-trial -->
New members get a 30 day free trial. We email a reminder 3 days before the trial ends. If you do not cancel before it ends, the membership starts and your default card is charged. Each household can use one trial.

# Cancelling Fernwick Plus
<!-- id: plus-cancel -->
Cancel any time under Account, then Membership, then Cancel. Benefits continue until the end of the period you have paid for, and the membership does not renew.

# Membership fee refunds
<!-- id: plus-refund -->
An annual membership fee is refunded in full if you cancel within 14 days of the charge and have not used any Plus benefit in that period. Monthly fees are not refunded.

# Pausing membership
<!-- id: plus-pause -->
Monthly members can pause Fernwick Plus for up to 3 months once per year under Account, then Membership. Annual memberships cannot be paused.

# Sharing benefits
<!-- id: plus-sharing -->
You can share shipping benefits with one other adult at the same address under Account, then Membership, then Share. Store credit earned is not shared.

# Switching between monthly and annual
<!-- id: plus-switch -->
Change your billing frequency under Account, then Membership. The change takes effect at your next renewal date.

# Plus store credit rewards
<!-- id: plus-rewards -->
The 5% reward is added as store credit 30 days after delivery, once the return window used for rewards has passed. Returned items do not earn rewards.

# Student discount
<!-- id: student-discount -->
Verified students get Fernwick Plus for $24 per year. Verify with a school email address under Account, then Membership, then Student.
