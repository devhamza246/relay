What is this?

Relay is a customer support agent for an online store. It answers routine questions from the store's help center, checks the signed-in customer's orders, opens tickets, and passes the conversation to a person when it should not handle it alone.

Problems it solves: support teams answering the same questions all day, chatbots that make things up or argue with an upset customer, and handoffs where the customer has to explain everything again. Relay cites its source on every answer, says when it does not know, and gives the human a summary and the full transcript.

How to use it

  1. Ask. Press a button under “Try it”, or open the chat at the bottom right and type your own question.
  2. Check the source. Click a number in a reply to read the help article or order record behind it.
  3. See why. “What the agent decided” shows how the message was classified, what was retrieved and how long it took.
  4. Trigger a handoff. Press “Billing dispute”, ask for a person, or ask something it cannot answer and then reply “yes” to the offer. Two failed answers also hand off.
  5. Be the support agent. “What the human agent receives” shows the ticket, summary, suspected intent and transcript.
